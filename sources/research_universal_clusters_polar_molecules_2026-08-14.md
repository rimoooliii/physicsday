## Experimental and theoretical context

- Xing-Yan Chen et al., “Ultracold field-linked tetratomic molecules,” Nature 626, 283–287 (2024), DOI 10.1038/s41586-023-06986-6. The experiment associated microwave-dressed NaK dimers into roughly 1.1e3 weakly bound tetramers, reported a maximum free-space lifetime of 8(2) ms, and imaged the anisotropic momentum-space wavefunction. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06986-6
- Tijs Karman and Jeremy M. Hutson, “Microwave Shielding of Ultracold Polar Molecules,” Phys. Rev. Lett. 121, 163401 (2018), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.121.163401. Establishes long-range repulsive microwave shielding and suppression of short-range loss. https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.121.163401
- Tijs Karman and Jeremy M. Hutson, “Microwave shielding of ultracold polar molecules with imperfectly circular polarization,” Phys. Rev. A 100, 052704 (2019), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevA.100.052704. Coupled-channel calculations show the tension between ellipticity and shielding efficiency. https://journals.aps.org/pra/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevA.100.052704
- Joy Dutta, Bijit Mukherjee, and Jeremy M. Hutson, “Universality in the microwave shielding of ultracold polar molecules,” Phys. Rev. Research 7, 023164 (2025), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevResearch.7.023164. Demonstrates near-universal two-body collision properties in reduced units, with deviations at very large Rabi frequency. https://journals.aps.org/prresearch/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.7.023164
- M. Olshanii, “Atomic Scattering in the Presence of an External Confinement and a Gas of Impenetrable Bosons,” Phys. Rev. Lett. 81, 938 (1998), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.81.938. Derives low-energy one-dimensional scattering under transverse confinement and the confinement-induced resonance. https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.81.938
- T. Bergeman, M. G. Moore, and M. Olshanii, “Atom-Atom Scattering under Cylindrical Harmonic Confinement: Numerical and Analytic Studies of the Confinement Induced Resonance,” Phys. Rev. Lett. 91, 163201 (2003), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.91.163201. Interprets the CIR as a Feshbach-type resonance between transverse modes and validates it with finite-range potentials. https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.91.163201
- M. D. Girardeau, “Relationship between Systems of Impenetrable Bosons and Fermions in One Dimension,” Journal of Mathematical Physics 1, 516–523 (1960). Establishes the one-to-one Bose–Fermi mapping for one-dimensional systems with an impenetrable core. DOI 10.1063/1.1703687.
- Ruijin Liu, Tingting Shi, Matteo Zaccanti, and Xiaoling Cui, “Universal clusters in quasi-two-dimensional ultracold Fermi mixtures,” Phys. Rev. Research 6, L042004 (2024), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevResearch.6.L042004. Develops the effective-2D cluster description that the second target preprint generalizes to unequal confinement frequencies and q1D. https://journals.aps.org/prresearch/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.6.L042004

## Audit cautions

- The first target paper validates the reduced model directly against exact three-dimensional calculations for two molecules, but the identical-three-molecule hexamer is solved only in the effective one-dimensional model. Its three-body three-dimensional benchmark is a Born–Oppenheimer heavy-heavy-light problem.
- The second target paper validates the low-dimensional effective parameters against the exact confined two-body bound state. Its trimer and tetramer energies are then obtained from effective low-dimensional STM equations, not a full confined three- or four-body calculation.
- Both target manuscripts are preprints in the supplied versions. Claims about experiments and prior published results above were checked against primary publisher or arXiv pages.
